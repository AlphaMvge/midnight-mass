# Midnight Mass — Brand Kit & Master Creative Brief

> **Version 2.1 — Uniform Brand System for Owners & Admins**

This master guide consolidates the visual identity, typography, color palette, asset directory, and media rules for the **Midnight Mass** Discord server.

---

## 1. Core Brand Identity

- **Tagline**: *“A late-night sanctuary for the sleepless. Dark aesthetics. Real conversations. No dead chats. No fake energy.”*
- **Vibe**: Gothic nocturnal sanctuary. Midnight church service meets underground dark-neon haven. Sacred, intimate, quiet intensity.
- **Tone**: Intimate, mysterious, direct, calm authority.
- **Official Invite Link**: `discord.gg/midnightmass`

---

## 2. Visual Identity & Imagery Rules

### Primary Visual Motifs
1. **Blackletter / Gothic Title Typography**: Staggered 3-line title arrangement ("The", "Midnight", "Mass") with tri-color gradient glow.
2. **Church Steeple & Full Moon**: Silhouetted spire against a glowing moon backdrop.
3. **Tri-Color Gradient Glow**: Crimson Rose (`#E11D48`) → Neon Purple (`#8B5CF6`) → Electric Blue (`#2563EB`).
4. **Dark Cathedral Aesthetic**: Veiled figures, glowing halos, subtle crimson blood-drip text accents.
5. **Official CTA Link**: `discord.gg/midnightmass` integrated into public promotional artwork.

---

---

## 4. Typography Hierarchy

1. **Title / Wordmarks**: `UnifrakturMaguntia` or `Cloister Black` (Blackletter Gothic font).
2. **Subheadings / Section Headers / Links**: `Cinzel` (Gothic serif, uppercase, wide tracking).
3. **Body Text / UI Copy**: `Inter` (Modern clean sans-serif).
