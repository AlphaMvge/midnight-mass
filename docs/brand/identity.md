## 🔤 Typography Standards

1. **Title Typography**: `Cinzel` or `UnifrakturMaguntia` (Blackletter / Gothic font).
2. **Subheadings**: `Cinzel` (Gothic serif, uppercase, wide letter spacing).
3. **Body Copy**: `Inter` (Clean modern sans-serif).
