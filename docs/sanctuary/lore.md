## 🖤 The Midnight Mass Vision

Midnight Mass was founded as a refuge from the noisy, transactional chaos of modern social media. We cater to night owls, dark aesthetic lovers, darkwave musicians, alternative fashion enthusiasts, and deep thinkers seeking real conversation at late hours.

### Core Pillars

1. **No Dead Chats, No Fake Energy**: Every interaction within the sanctuary is intentional, respectful, and authentic.
2. **Gothic Cyberpunk Aesthetic**: Illuminated blackletter typography, neon purple halos, gold accents, and dark atmospheric warmth.
3. **Inclusive Nocturnal Haven**: A sanctuary for sleepless creatives, alternative subcultures, and late-night intellectual banter.
