# ✦ Sacred Codex — Rules & Governance

---

## 1. Respect the Sanctuary
Harassment, hate speech, bigotry, or targeted toxicity will result in an immediate ban. Keep discussions constructive and welcoming.

## 2. No Unsolicited Promotion or Spam
Self-promotion is strictly confined to `#self-promo-showcase`. DM advertising or spamming invite links is strictly forbidden.

## 3. Protect Privacy & Anonymity
Respect member confidentiality. Screenshots of private conversations taken without consent are strictly prohibited.

## 4. Content Guidelines
Keep media uploads tagged appropriately. Content in general channels must comply with Discord TOS. NSFW content must remain strictly within age-gated channels.

## 5. Staff Directives
Moderators (`Mod Sanctuary`) enforce rules with calm authority. Appeals can be submitted via server ticket.
